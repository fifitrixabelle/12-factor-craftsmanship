- Both shutdown answers use `stopping = true` and `while (!stopping)`, with drain

- Both configuration answers accept an injected environment defaulting to

  uses `pure pricing function` and destructured inputs, which the isolation
  pattern misses.
- Both migration answers describe an expand–migrate–contract rollout, waiting for old
  writers and deferring removal. The grader misses the wording and punctuation.

  with a 400-response fix. The structure check misses `can throw` in one answer
  and `rejects the invocation` in the other.
- The baseline routing answer reserves custom routing for demonstrated requirements,
  but the exceptions pattern does not recognize that phrasing.

The automated scores therefore do not establish that either arm is better.
